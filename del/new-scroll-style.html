About Me

I do the full software development lifecycle, but what pulls me most is the backend — the logic, the systems, the way things actually work under the hood. That curiosity started before I ever wrote a line of code.

One of my projects, Sydny, is a local desktop voice assistant powered by Llama — built entirely from scratch. No cloud, no subscriptions, no API keys. Just a model running on local hardware, a microphone, and a lot of Python.

Outside of code, I'm a filmmaker. Travel videos, music videos, short films. It bleeds into how I think about interfaces — I approach UI the way a cinematographer approaches a frame.

I got into programming at 22 with no CS background — just YouTube tutorials and a lot of frustration. Moving from the Philippines to Nova Scotia gave me the push I needed to learn something practical. What I didn't expect was how deep the rabbit hole would go.

The shift that changed everything: I stopped asking "how do I do X" and started asking "why does X work this way." That's when I stopped feeling like someone who could type code and started feeling like a developer.

Right now I'm working toward a software internship where I can go deep on backend work, contribute to a real codebase, and keep building things that matter. If that sounds like your team, I'd love to talk.
